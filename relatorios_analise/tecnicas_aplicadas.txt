Técnicas aplicadas:
- Ensemble Feature Selection: RFE (Random Forest) e f_classif (univariate)
- Propensity Score Matching: regressão logística para estimar propensity score
- Referências:
  - Ensemble Feature Selection: Saeys et al. (2007), Abeel et al. (2010), Song et al. (2018)
  - Propensity Score Matching: Rosenbaum & Rubin (1983), Stürmer et al. (2005)
